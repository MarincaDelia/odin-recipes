Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made of layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy bechamel and melted cheese.

It takes some time to prepare, but it is perfect for family dinners and tastes even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 700g tomato passata
  • 250g mozzarella, grated
  • salt, pepper and basil
  • 500ml bechamel sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic, add the beef and cook until browned.
  3. Add the passata, salt, pepper and basil, then simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Spread a layer of sauce in a baking dish, then add lasagna sheets.
  5. Add meat sauce, béchamel and mozzarella. Repeat the layers.
  6. Finish with béchamel and mozzarella on top.
  7. Bake for 40 minutes until golden, then let it rest for 10 minutes.
